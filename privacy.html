Cache Rider privacy policy

Applies to the Cache Rider website (cacherider.com), the Android app, and the watchface companion. Last updated 27 September 2026.

The short version

Nothing about you leaves your device. Cache Rider has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking of any kind. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

Location

If you choose to share your location, the app uses it on your device only, to sort stops by distance, to point the compass at a stop, and to show where you are on the map. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in your device's settings.

What the app fetches

The app downloads the bus timetable, the map and service alerts from cacherider.com, and live bus positions from a relay at live.cacherider.com that reads the transit district's public realtime feed. These requests carry no identifying information beyond what any web request carries (your device's network address, which is not stored or logged by the app). The app never sends your location, your saved stops or anything you type to any server.

What stays on your device

Your saved stops, recent stops, settings, the timetable and the offline map are kept in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. They are yours; clearing the site's data removes them.

Links to other apps

"Walk there" opens your device's own maps app with a stop as the destination. What happens there is governed by that app's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear on this page with a new date. Questions go to the address on the app's Google Play listing, or open an issue at github.com/sybenx/cacherider.

Cache Rider is unofficial and is not affiliated with the Cache Valley Transit District or Utah State University.
