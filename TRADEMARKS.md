The code in this repository is published to be read, all rights reserved (see `LICENSE`). Whatever permission its
maker gives for the code, it doesn't cover the name or the look that says an app is this one.

**Cache Rider**, the Cache Rider icon and the cacherider.com name belong to its maker, and aren't licensed at all.
They say "this is the app its maker builds and keeps up to date", and a copy that uses them says something that
isn't so.

## What's welcome

- With a licence for the code, publishing your own version **under another name and icon**.
- Saying your version is "based on Cache Rider" or "a fork of Cache Rider", with a link here.
- Using the name to talk about Cache Rider itself: reviews, articles, links, a recommendation to a friend.
- Building the Android app from this repository for your own use, the icon and all, as long as you don't publish it.

## What needs asking first

- Publishing an app, site or listing called Cache Rider, or anything close enough to be taken for it, on the Play
  Store, the App Store or anywhere else.
- Using the Cache Rider icon, or one made to look like it, for something published.
- Suggesting Cache Rider made, supports or approves your version when it doesn't.

Ask at feedback@cacherider.com. For most things the answer is yes.
