## Full description

Cache Rider tells you when the next bus leaves the stop you're standing at, in
Logan and across Cache Valley, Utah. Made by a rider, not by the transit
district.

WHAT IT DOES

• The next bus, first. Open the app and the answer is the biggest thing on the
  screen: the next departure from your saved stop, or from the stop nearest
  you, with a countdown when it's close.
• Live. Once a bus is on the road its position and its expected time at every
  stop come straight from the transit district's own tracker. Rows say Live
  instead of Scheduled, with the scheduled time crossed out beside the real one.
• Every stop. Search by street, number, town or stop number, or type any
  address on the valley's grid and get the stops nearest it. The stop across
  the road, for the other direction, is one tap away.
• The map. Every stop and every route on a Cache Valley street map, buses
  moving on it, and the next buses for any stop you tap. Save the map for
  offline.
• The Transit Center, bay by bay: the next time the routes leave together,
  which buses are in, and everything leaving in the next hour.
• Detours and closed stops, from the transit district's alerts, on the stop,
  the route and the map.
• The USU Aggie Shuttle, live: each campus stop shows how many stops away the
  next shuttle is and about how many minutes.
• A compass on every stop page: which way the stop is and how far, and a
  Walk there link that opens your maps app.
• Works offline once it has loaded the timetable. Dark mode follows your phone.

PRIVACY

Nothing about you leaves your phone. No account, no analytics, no cookies, no
tracking. Your location, if you share it, is used on the device only to sort
stops by distance. The app talks to the timetable and the live bus feed and
nothing else.

FREE AND OPEN

The same app is free on the web at cacherider.com, and its source is public.
This listing is the convenient way to have it on your home screen.

Cache Rider is unofficial. It is not affiliated with the Cache Valley Transit
District or Utah State University. Times are the published schedule and the
district's live estimates; buses can still run early or late.
